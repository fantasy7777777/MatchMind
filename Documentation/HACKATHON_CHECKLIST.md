# Hackathon checklist

This checklist is transcribed from the overview and the `OFFICIAL RULES.md` supplied by the project owner. The rules say the Official Rules prevail over conflicting promotional materials and may be amended. Confirm the current requirements on the official platform before submission.

## Project direction

- [ ] New project created after the start of the registration period.
- [ ] Project uses synthetic, football-realistic data for the hackathon entry.
- [ ] Project fits at least one prize category and demonstrates the relevant category criteria.
- [ ] Third-party software, SDKs, APIs, data, footage, marks, and other assets are used only with appropriate rights and permissions.

## Submission package

- [ ] Project pitch explains what was built, technologies used, what it does, and the problem it solves.
- [ ] Demonstration video is **under two minutes**.
- [ ] Video shows the project functioning on its intended device/platform.
- [ ] Video is hosted at a public URL on an accepted platform listed in the rules.
- [ ] Video contains no unlicensed third-party marks, copyrighted music, or other copyrighted material.
- [ ] Public GitHub repository URL is included.
- [ ] Working project link, demo, or test build is provided for judging; include credentials if needed.
- [ ] Project remains free and available for judging/testing through the judging period.
- [ ] Submission materials and testing instructions are in English or include English translations.
- [ ] Submission is original, owned by the entrant/team, and has not won another contest.
- [ ] Team size is at most four and a representative is authorized to submit.

## Dates listed in the supplied materials

- Registration deadline: **October 20, 2026, 12:00 PM Pacific Time**.
- Submission deadline: **October 27, 2026, 11:59 PM Pacific Time**.
- Judging period in the rules: through **November 10, 2026, 11:59 PM Pacific Time**.
- Rules say winners will be announced no later than **November 20, 2026**. The overview separately says the top six projects will be selected/notified by November 10; confirm this milestone on the platform.

## Judging emphasis

Stage one is a baseline pass/fail for viability, theme fit, and reasonable use of featured technologies. Stage two assigns equal weight (20% each) to:

- Technological implementation.
- Agentic design and innovation.
- Real-world impact and applicability.
- User experience and presentation.
- Adherence to the selected category.

The demo should make these qualities visible through one coherent user journey. Avoid adding technology solely for a checklist if it does not improve the product or demonstrable implementation.

## Recommended video story

Follow the current [two-minute demo script](DEMO_SCRIPT.md) and show only capabilities that exist in the submitted build. The standalone 5v5 page currently supports scenario selection, deterministic replay, timeline seeking, interpolated player/ball movement, and visible ball-state transitions. Label the scenarios as simulated.

Do not build the video around forecasts, AI explanations, live statistics, referee distance, personalization controls, or analyst output unless those features are implemented, tested, and present in the final submission. A polished replay of one complete scenario is stronger than describing unimplemented plans.

The supplied rules state entrants retain ownership of submissions, while granting the Promotion Entities a worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, fully paid, non-exclusive license to use submissions for commercial or non-commercial purposes. Review the full rules before entering if this grant affects your plans.
