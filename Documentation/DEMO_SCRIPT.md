# MatchMind two-minute demo video script

## Goal and timing

Introduce MatchMind through the viewer and broadcast problems it aims to address, then demonstrate all three 5v5 scenes in a video no longer than 2:00. Keep the delivery conversational and let the on-screen action carry the story. The narration is approximately 280 words; the timecodes include brief interaction holds.

The prototype uses authored synthetic sequences and illustrative supplementary values. Do not describe the current experience as live match analysis, AI-generated commentary, calibrated prediction, or real tracking.

## Recording notes

- Keep the final edit at or below 2:00, including title cards and transitions.
- Use a readable desktop-sized viewport; keep the selected scenario, clock, pitch, and controls visible.
- Use playback for movement, then seek when a specific moment or detail needs to be held on screen.
- Expand the roster only briefly. Keep optional details collapsed until their scenario marker is shown.
- Keep voiceover measured and leave enough time for the viewer to see the pass, foul, corner flight, and header.
- If a playback or transition takes longer than planned, shorten pauses rather than speaking faster.

## Claims to avoid

- Do not claim real match data, live event ingestion, continuous optical tracking, or current AI-generated narratives or forecasts.
- Player ratings, shot speed, xG, and possible next actions are illustrative authored content, not measured or calibrated outputs.
- Do not imply referee-distance measurement, full-match statistics, multilingual delivery, or production-system integration.

## Judging traceability

- **Viewer experience:** selectable short scenes, roster, optional information layers, pitch-linked details, and manual seeking.
- **Technical demonstration:** deterministic player and ball movement, ball flight paths, event timing, and explicit scenario outcomes.
- **Real-world direction:** a viewer-first concept for adding context to football coverage without forcing every detail onto the screen.
- **Honesty about scope:** synthetic, authored prototype content is clearly distinguished from future live and AI-powered capabilities.
